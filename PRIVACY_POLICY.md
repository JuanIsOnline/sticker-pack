# Privacy Policy for iOS Sticker Packs

**Effective Date:** August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all standalone iOS / iMessage Sticker Pack applications developed by Juan Haber, including **L & Goku** and related sticker packs hosted in this repository.

Our sticker pack applications do not collect, store, track, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, device information, message content, or analytics.

Our sticker pack applications do not collect any personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13.
